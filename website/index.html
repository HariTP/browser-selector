Ask a question ↗
Which browsers and profiles work?+

Chrome, Chromium, Brave, Edge, and Vivaldi support profile selection, along with detected Firefox profiles. Safari and other detected browsers can open links, but Safari profile selection and Firefox containers aren’t supported. Profiles must be in standard locations.

Does it see my browsing history?+

No. Browser Selector reads browser and profile names, not your cookies, passwords, or browsing history. Links aren’t logged or sent to a service.

Is it always running?+

No background service is needed. Your system opens Browser Selector when another app sends a web link to your default browser. It shows the chooser and passes the link to your selection.

Will it catch every link?+

It handles HTTP and HTTPS links opened through your system default browser. Links inside a browser stay there. Apps with built-in browsers or their own browser preferences can bypass the chooser.

Can I switch back?+

Yes. Choose your usual browser in your system’s default-app settings whenever you want. Browser Selector doesn’t replace or modify your browsers.
